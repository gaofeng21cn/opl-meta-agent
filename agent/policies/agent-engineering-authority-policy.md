# Agent Engineering Authority Policy

Agent design truth, target assessment, blueprint acceptance, evaluation quality, and evolution decisions remain owned by OPL Meta Agent. OPL transports refs and receipts; it does not infer target readiness from provider completion.

Keep reusable agent-engineering methods in the product knowledge pack. Owner,
topic and project knowledge retain one canonical source chosen by their owners:
an existing personal or team library, ordinary workspace Markdown, or both for
different knowledge objects. Framework binds authorized sources and immutable
consumption evidence; the target domain owns semantic acceptance and writeback.
Execution records do not replace business artifacts or accepted knowledge.
